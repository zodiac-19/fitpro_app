Privacy Policy for FitPro

Last updated: November 5, 2026

FitPro ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Your Name. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, who can see it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account: The App does not require you to create an account or log in with an email or password. The App creates an anonymous identifier so that features such as Squads can work. When you first open the App, you enter your age, weight, and height.

b) Camera and Photos: The App asks for camera permission, or access to your gallery, so you can add a photo of your body for assessment. When you request an assessment, the photo is uploaded over an encrypted connection to Firebase Storage (Google) and sent to an artificial intelligence service for analysis. The photo is deleted from our servers immediately after the analysis result is returned. The photo itself is never shared with other users and is never stored in Squads. The AI assessment result (such as estimated body fat percentage, body type, and muscle scores) is saved in the App and may be shared with your Squad, as described below. You can deny or revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings, but the assessment feature will not work without it.

c) Squads and Shared Data: The App includes a Squads (team) feature. Sharing is your choice. It happens only when you send your invite code to a friend, or enter a friend's invite code in the App. Once two users are linked, each can see the other's information. The following information is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) and is visible to the other member of your Squad: your display name, your Squad invite code, your age, weight, height, estimated body fat percentage, body type, muscle scores and rankings, workout sessions, calories burned, best lifts, training progress, and your goals. Photos are never included. Please do not use your real name as your display name if you prefer not to be identified. You can leave a Squad at any time by using the Unlink button, which removes your data from the Squad.

d) Health and body-related data: Your age, weight, height, body measurements, estimated body fat, and workout information are health and fitness data and may be sensitive. We use them only to provide the App's features, including assessments, plans, goals, and Squads. We do not sell this data.

e) Local data: Some data, such as settings, your plan, and your assessment history, is stored on your device.

f) Automatically collected data: Through Firebase and Google AdMob, we may automatically collect device information, Advertising ID, app instance ID, IP address, app usage, and crash and diagnostics data.

2. How We Use Information
- To provide AI-based body assessment results.
- To create workout plans, goals, and tasks.
- To let you and your Squad friend see each other's progress.
- To maintain, secure, and improve the App.
- To show advertisements and measure their performance.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing
To analyze your photos, the App uses third-party AI services: Google Gemini (Google) and Grok (xAI). Your photo is sent to one of these services only when you request an assessment. These providers process the data under their own terms and privacy policies. Assessments are automated estimates and may be inaccurate.

4. Advertising
The App uses Google AdMob to show banner and other ads. AdMob may use the Advertising ID and other data to show and measure ads, which may be personalized. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads). In the EEA/UK, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Third-Party Services
- Firebase (Google): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Google AdMob and Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- xAI (Grok): https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy

6. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. Your Squad data is shared with the user you link with through an invite code. Other data is shared only with the service providers listed above, solely to operate the App and show ads. We may disclose data if required by law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion
Photos are deleted from our servers immediately after analysis. Squad data is kept on our servers until you remove it. Squad data is removed when you unlink or when you contact us. You can also request deletion of all your data by emailing your-email@example.com, and we will respond within 30 days. Data stored only on your device is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

8. Security
Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored using Firebase security measures. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Anyone who has your Squad invite code and enters it can link with you and see your Squad data, so share it only with people you trust.

9. Not Medical Advice
The App provides general fitness-related estimates for informational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified professional before changing your diet or exercise routine.

10. Children's Privacy
The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights
Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at the email below.

12. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
